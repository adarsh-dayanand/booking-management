Careful where it counts.

Bookings, not medicine

The agent never gives medical advice. If someone describes an emergency, it points them to emergency services and hands the chat to your staff.

Your calendar stays private

Neem asks Google for free and busy time only. Personal events and their details are never read.

Each clinic kept separate

Questions clinics ask.

What is Neem?

Neem is an appointment booking assistant for clinics. It replies to patients on WhatsApp or website chat, offers only the times that are free on your Google Calendar, takes payment through Razorpay if you want it, and confirms the booking.

Do patients need to install anything?

No. They use WhatsApp as they already do, or the chat bubble on your website. Nothing to download and no account to create.

What if the agent cannot help?

It hands the conversation to your staff. Urgent or clinical questions are never answered by the agent.

Can the doctor approve each booking?

Yes. In approval mode the doctor receives a WhatsApp request and replies APPROVE or REJECT with a reason. Switch to instant booking whenever you like.

Can patients pay when they book?

Yes, through Razorpay. Your admin connects it, turns on payment for the services you choose and sets the amount. Patients pay inside WhatsApp and the slot is confirmed.

Does it work with my existing calendar?

It connects to Google Calendar. The doctor sends one message to authorize it, and revoking access pauses automatic confirmation.

Can it answer questions about my clinic?

Yes. You write the clinic information and the agent answers from that text, such as timings, location and services.
