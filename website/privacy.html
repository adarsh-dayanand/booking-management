Privacy Policy

Effective 25 October 2026 · Last updated 25 October 2026

Neem is an appointment booking assistant for clinics. It answers patients on WhatsApp and website chat, books them into the clinic's calendar, and sends reminders and alerts. This policy explains what information Neem handles, why, who it is shared with, and the choices you have. In this policy, "Neem", "we" and "us" mean [Legal entity name] ("the Operator"), which runs the Neem service. "Clinic" means a clinic, doctor or other business that uses Neem. "Patient" means a person who chats with a clinic through Neem.

1. Who is responsible for your data

Patients. The clinic you book with decides why and how your booking details are used, and Neem processes them on the clinic's behalf and on its instructions. For requests about your booking data, contact the clinic first. We will help the clinic respond.

Clinic staff and website visitors. For the account details of clinic staff, and for people who send us a demo request, Neem decides how the information is used.

2. Information we handle

Patients and chat users

  • Phone number, and your WhatsApp profile name when you message us on WhatsApp.
  • Details you choose to share in the chat: name, email address, date of birth, preferred language.
  • The messages in the conversation, and the appointments you book, change or cancel (service, doctor, date and time, status, reference).
  • Payment status and references for fees paid through Razorpay. We never see or store your card, UPI or bank details.
  • One-time verification codes sent to your WhatsApp number to prove it is yours.

Please do not share medical details in the chat. The assistant is instructed not to collect or store medical information and is not a way to describe symptoms or seek advice. Because chat messages are free text, anything you type is stored as part of the conversation.

Clinic staff and clinic settings

  • Login email address and a password stored only as a one-way hash.
  • Clinic name, time zone, services, doctors, working hours, clinic information for the assistant to answer from, and the staff WhatsApp number.
  • Connection credentials you give us (for example Google Calendar, WhatsApp and Razorpay keys). These are stored encrypted.

Demo requests

Your name, clinic name and WhatsApp number, if you submit the form on our website.

Technical information

Server logs such as IP address, time, and error and delivery records (for example whether a WhatsApp message was delivered). The clinic dashboard keeps a login token in your browser so you stay signed in.

3. How we use information

  • To run the service: check availability, make, change and cancel bookings, and answer questions using the clinic's own information.
  • To send booking confirmations, reminders, payment updates, and alerts to the clinic's staff by WhatsApp and email.
  • To verify phone numbers, keep accounts secure, prevent abuse and fix problems.
  • To respond to demo requests and support questions, and to meet legal obligations.

We do not sell personal information, and we do not use patient conversations or calendar data for advertising.

4. Google user data

A clinic's doctor may connect their Google Calendar. When they do, we ask for two permissions:

  • calendar.freebusy: to see when the doctor is busy or free, so only free times are offered to patients.
  • calendar.events.owned: to create, update, delete and read the appointment events that Neem puts on the doctor's own calendar, and to notice if the doctor moves or removes one.

We use this data only to provide those features to that doctor and their clinic. The assistant is given only the resulting free time slots; calendar event contents are not sent to AI model providers. Access tokens are stored encrypted. We do not use Google user data for advertising, we do not sell it, and we do not allow people to read it except where needed for security, to comply with law, or with the account owner's consent.

Neem's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

You can revoke access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Once revoked, Neem can no longer reach the calendar. Contact us to have the stored connection details deleted.

5. Who we share information with

We share information only with service providers that are needed to run Neem, and only as much as they need:

  • Meta (WhatsApp Business Platform): to send and receive WhatsApp messages.
  • An AI model provider: chat text and the clinic's information are sent to the provider the service is configured to use (Google Gemini, OpenAI or Anthropic) so the assistant can reply. Providers process this to generate the reply under their own terms.
  • Google: for Calendar, as described above.
  • Razorpay: to create payment links and confirm payments. Payments are made to the clinic's own Razorpay account.
  • Resend: to deliver email alerts to the clinic's staff, which contain the patient's name, phone number and appointment details.
  • Hosting and database providers: that store and run the service for us.

Booking details are also visible to the clinic you booked with and its authorised staff. We may disclose information if the law requires it, or to protect the rights, safety or security of users or the service. If the business is sold or reorganised, information may transfer to the new owner under this policy.

Some of these providers process information outside India.

6. How long we keep it

We keep conversations, patient profiles and appointment records for as long as the clinic's account is active, so the clinic can see its history. When a clinic ends its account, or on a verified deletion request, we delete or anonymise the data within 30 days, except what we must keep for legal, tax, security or dispute reasons. Backups are overwritten on their normal cycle.

7. Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections. Connection credentials and tokens are encrypted when stored, passwords are hashed, each clinic can only see its own data, and unsigned or forged webhook requests are rejected. No system is perfectly secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security. If we learn of a breach that affects you, we will notify those affected and the authorities as the law requires.

8. Your rights and choices

Under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and other applicable law, you may ask to access your information, correct it, have it erased, withdraw consent, and raise a grievance. You can also stop chatting at any time. To exercise a right, contact the clinic you booked with, or write to us at the address below and tell us which phone number and clinic are involved. We may need to verify that the number is yours. We will respond within a reasonable time and in any case as the law requires. Withdrawing consent or erasing data may mean we can no longer manage your bookings.

9. Children

Neem is meant to be used by adults. If a parent or guardian books for a child, they should be the person using the chat, and the clinic is responsible for getting any guardian consent the law requires.

10. Cookies and similar technologies

The clinic dashboard uses browser storage only to keep you signed in. We do not use advertising cookies. Our website may load fonts and icons from third parties, which can see your IP address when your browser requests them.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version, and for significant changes we will notify clinics through the dashboard or by email.
